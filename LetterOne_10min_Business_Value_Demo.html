One platform, four teams

Show

Show
The four AI agents in Snowflake Intelligence: Treasury Ops, Risk, Structured Credit, Direct Lending.

2:30 – 4:30

Treasury Operations

For David Talbot (COO), Steven Gladstone, Dmitry Yurin

Show
The Ops control dashboard (Streamlit): P&L, cash rec, cash forecast, position rec. Each shows complete, running or exception. The Excel checklist, replaced.
Show
One reconciliation break: AI explains the likely cause in plain English. Rules in SQL, not VBA.
Ask the Treasury Ops Agent
"What is our total cash by bank counterparty and currency this morning, and what changed versus yesterday?"
Then open
The generated SQL and the verified query underneath the answer. Show where the number comes from.
Say

"Add a new bank account or entity by adding one row. No new spreadsheet, no new macro. Growth without adding people."

Value: Ops headcount time on report production, month-end coordination (David's 75% estimate), plus the reconciliation vendor fee.
4:30 – 6:30

Risk

For Jason Lai (CRO), Sam Drury

Show
Daily Enfusion snapshots building a full time series. Data quality checks surface only breaches — no more dump-and-compare.
Ask the Risk Agent
"Show USD-equivalent exposure by entity."
Say

"This is the same question I asked the Ops agent. Same number. One definition in the semantic layer, one answer no matter who asks."

Show
Monte Carlo VaR in a Snowflake Notebook: parameters visible (lookback, decay, confidence, paths). Change one assumption — the correlation matrix — and the result moves. Persist it, schedule it daily. "Check once, reuse forever."
Value: 20–30% of analyst time recaptured from assembly and checking. Time to answer a new question drops from days to minutes.
6:30 – 8:30

Trading — Structured Credit

For Amy Abob, Matei Moprea

Show
One structured credit ledger: Enfusion + Intex + Bloomberg DL+. One governed row per tranche.
Ask the Structured Credit Agent
"What is our exposure by CLO manager and rating, and how has it changed since last month's marks?"
The wow moment
Drop a trustee report PDF into a stage. AI_EXTRACT pulls OC/IC cushions, WARF, CCC bucket. Ask: "Which deals saw the CCC bucket rise by more than one point?" — PDF to trended, queryable data in minutes. No Excel step.
Say

"Same question, same answer, no matter who on the desk asks. That's because it comes from one semantic view with verified queries — not a different Perplexity session each time."

Value: 10–20% manual time recaptured across three people. Capacity to cover new asset classes — a revenue story, not just cost.
8:30 – 9:30

Direct Lending

For Daniele Gualdani

Show
A synthetic deal folder pointed at Snowflake. AI classifies every file: model, IM, valuation, legal, monitoring.
Show
Model-vs-IM check: AI_EXTRACT pulls key figures from both. Mismatches flagged to the source page and cell. A Q&A list for the counterparty is drafted automatically.
Say

"This is what Daniele does with ChatGPT today — but governed, logged, repeatable, and sitting next to 100+ deals of institutional memory."

Governance moment
Switch to a Trading role and ask about a live deal. Access is refused. Deal data is visible only to Direct Lending.
Value: 30%+ of time on validation. Review cut from 3 days to 1. More deals assessed with the same team.
